Snootme: connecting communication and scheduling

At Snootme, I worked across the application and its integrations. I integrated video calling features, screen sharing, image-editing plugins and automated scheduling through calendar integration. My full-stack contribution also included AI and Nylas work. The product problem was connecting communication and productivity capabilities in a usable application.

I handled the integration work that connected these capabilities. The resulting functionality supported calls, shared screens and calendar-based scheduling in the product. This is the part of forward deployed engineering that fits my experience: working across product features and external systems to deliver a complete business workflow.

Summit Crew: turning operational needs into APIs and CRM workflows

At Summit Crew, I designed and developed APIs, models and CRM components for work including Boost Summit, Chit Fund and Little Italy BIA. I used scenario-based MVPs and spec-driven development, with delivery workflows managed through Jira and Linear. My contribution covered both the application structure and the business features built on top of it.

I also refactored legacy handlers and optimized SQL and NoSQL database operations. Those changes reduced maintenance overhead and query latency across core applications. The work connected product delivery with the less visible improvements that make an application easier to maintain and more responsive.

Email routing: automating a repetitive operational task

At Cybernef Technologies, I engineered an n8n pipeline integrated with Outlook and Supabase. I added a React dashboard that provided visibility into intelligent email routing. My work covered the automation and the interface used to understand its activity.

The outcome was an automated routing workflow with a corresponding dashboard. This illustrates how I handle operational software: I connect the task to the systems involved and provide a way for the team to see what the workflow is doing. The implemented capabilities are email routing, data integration and operational visibility.
