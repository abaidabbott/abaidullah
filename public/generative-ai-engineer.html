The AutoBot: connecting vector search and knowledge graphs

I worked on The AutoBot as a full-stack AI engineer. The assistant combines vector search and knowledge graphs in a GraphRAG application. My project record identifies GraphRAG and Neo4j as part of that work. The business focus is retrieving information for an enterprise assistant.

The implemented capability is an assistant that uses both vector-based retrieval and graph-based knowledge. This is a concrete example of my RAG architecture experience: connecting retrieval components to an application that users can interact with. It also connects my backend and AI work within the same product.

Pinecone and LangChain: improving semantic retrieval

At Cybernef Technologies, I designed a RAG system with Pinecone and LangChain to improve the accuracy of semantic data queries. This was information-retrieval work: bringing relevant material into the interaction so the application could respond using the underlying information.

I also worked on pytest coverage for RAG retrieval pipelines and configured observability tools. The optimization was therefore supported by engineering around the retrieval behavior, rather than being limited to prompt changes. My résumé records an improvement in semantic retrieval accuracy without a published numerical benchmark.

Planet AI and Twin Companion: two different AI workflows

On Planet AI, I worked on a full-stack workflow builder using multi-agent orchestration to automate business tasks. On Twin Companion, my role covered the frontend and AI integration for a conversational application with session memory and adaptive sentiment matching.

Planet AI demonstrates my work on coordinating AI-driven tasks. Twin Companion demonstrates conversational behavior and continuity. Together, they show why my agent architecture and application work spans different product needs: orchestrated business operations in one case and an ongoing conversational experience in the other.

Voice, documents and production engineering

My AI work at Cybernef included speech-to-text, text-to-speech and image generation using Hugging Face and OpenAI. Study Mind adds full-stack LLM work for document-based reasoning, notes and study scheduling. These are examples of connecting model capabilities to interfaces people can use.

My wider toolkit includes LangGraph, CrewAI, LlamaIndex, LangChain, RAG evaluation and observability tools.
